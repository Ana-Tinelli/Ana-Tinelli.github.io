01 / Academic analysis

UK Computing Career Outlook

Excel · Python · pandas · scikit-learn

How do five computing career areas compare in employment scale, projected growth and curriculum coverage?

Approximately 66,470 additional jobs projected for the software grouping between 2025 and 2035.

Explore the approach

What I did

Compared moving-average and exponential-smoothing forecasts using a common MSE validation period, examined regression relationships and explored K-means configurations. I contributed to the occupational mapping with a teammate and developed the individual Future Growth analysis.

What the evidence can tell us

Mixed occupational allocations use vacancy proxies. Short time series and small samples make modelling exploratory; association is not causation.

E-commerce SQL Analysis

SQL · SQLite · Python

How can relational data support useful analysis while protecting the integrity of application operations?

Four analytical queries, review constraints and a transactional checkout workflow.

Explore the approach

What I did

Combined two assessments: shopper and order queries, seller-product aggregates and category comparisons; then review-table design and a Python shopping application. Published aggregate outputs match the repository database.

03 / Academic analysis

Disneyland Reviews Analysis

Python · CSV · Matplotlib

How do review ratings differ across California, Hong Kong and Paris?

42,652 reviews analysed; average ratings of 4.41, 4.20 and 3.96 respectively.

Explore the approach

What I did

Built a modular command-line application with processing, interface, chart and export modules. Independent checks covered counts, means, temporal calculations and TXT, CSV and JSON exports.

What the evidence can tell us

The sample is not representative of every visitor. There are 2,613 missing dates; monthly averages pool years, and location rankings need sample-count context.

Professional case study / Experience-based postgraduate study

Evaluating inspection equipment for electronics manufacturing

I carried out the evaluation from defining criteria and conducting tests through comparison, recommendation and implementation. My postgraduate article explains the structured methods used to understand the process and support equipment evaluation.

Process mappingIshikawa analysisKPI definitionPugh matrix
Read the case study and evidence boundaries

The question

How could inspection equipment meet requirements for a process with approximately 700 product variants, around 30 daily setup changes and a shortest line cycle time of 20 seconds?

The approach

Map the process, investigate inspection limitations, define measurable requirements, test alternatives and compare them against the selected criteria.

Interpreting the evidence

These figures describe the process context, not measured improvements. The article’s 98% approval figure is a test target, not an achieved defect-detection rate. Its equipment-comparison scores are illustrative examples, not actual supplier results. No measured return on investment is claimed.
